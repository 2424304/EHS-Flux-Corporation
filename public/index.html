Water Treatment Plant Design

Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)

ETP Upgradation & Optimization

Compliance Support

Zero Liquid Discharge

Zero Liquid Discharge Solutions

We help industries design and implement practical ZLD systems that balance compliance, cost and operational reliability.

  • Wastewater characterization and stream segregation.
  • Pre-treatment system design.
  • RO system integration.
  • Multiple Effect Evaporator (MEE) support.
  • Mechanical Vapour Recompression (MVR) evaluation.
  • ATFD and crystallizer selection.
  • Energy and operating cost optimization.
  • Condensate recovery and reuse strategy.
